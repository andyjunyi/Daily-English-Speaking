# Daily English Speaking — 情境對話練習

## 1. 教室篇（Classroom）

**角色：** A = 愛炫耀的同學，B = 你

```
A: Dude, I finished the test in 10 minutes!
B: （Get over yourself.）
A: What? I'm just saying!
B: （The world doesn't revolve around you.）

---

A: I can't believe I failed again...
B: （Don't worry about it.）You'll do better next time.

---

A: (打斷別人說話，一直講)
B: （Read the room!）We're trying to study here.

---

A: Let's copy each other's homework.
B: （Hard pass!）I'm not getting detention for you.
```

---

## 2. 社團活動篇（Club Activity）

**角色：** A = 隊長，B = 隊員

```
A: We need someone to organize the booth.
B: （I've got this.）I'll take care of it.

A: Thanks! And can you print the flyers too?
B: （Leave it with me.）

---

A: This is too hard, I want to quit.
B: （It is what it is.）Just give it your best.

---

A: We have to stay after school tomorrow.
B: （Call it a day.）We've been working for 3 hours already!
```

---

## 3. 朋友聊天篇（Hanging Out）

**角色：** A, B = 好朋友

```
A: I just found out I didn't make the team.
B: （That hurts.）I'm so sorry, man.

---

A: Guess who showed up late again?
B: （Classic you.）You're always late!

---

A: (突然想起一件事) Oh! I totally forgot something!
B: （It just hit me.）What is it?

---

A: Do you want to go eat or watch a movie?
B: （Let's play it by ear.）We'll decide when we get there.
```

---

## 4. 家庭篇（Family）

**角色：** A = 爸媽，B = 你

```
A: Why did you only get 70 on the English test?
B: （Speak for yourself.）Your English wasn't great either when you were my age.

A: Don't talk back to me!
B: （Scratch that.）I'm sorry.

---

A: (一直唸你同一件事)
B: （Drop it!）I heard you the first time.

---

A: You spent all afternoon playing games?
B: （Forget about it.）I already finished my homework.
```

---

## 5. 餐廳 / 購物篇（Restaurant / Shopping）

**角色：** A = 店員，B = 顧客（你）

```
A: Is it expensive? I don't have much money.
B: （It's on the house.）Don't worry about it!

---

A: That drink looks really good.
B: （Perfect timing.）I just bought one — here, try some.

---

A: I don't think this fits me.
B: （It's spot on.）It looks great on you!

---

A: Thanks for helping me carry the bags.
B: （Much appreciated.）Sorry for making you carry so much.
```

---

## 6. 網路群組篇（Group Chat）

**角色：** 群組聊天

```
A: Guys, I think we should all study together tonight.
B: （Who's we?）I didn't agree to that.

A: Everyone is coming except you.
C: （Like you know!）Don't speak for everyone.

A: Fine, forget it.
C: （So sue me!）
```

---

## 練習方式

1. **角色扮演**：找一位同學分別扮演 A、B，輪流練習
2. **一人分飾兩角**：自己用兩種語氣練習
3. **改編挑戰**：把對話改編成你自己的真實生活情境
4. **錄音回聽**：用手機錄下來，聽聽自己唸得自不自然
